# Photos in the launch video

Four photos replace the loremflickr images in `public/landscaping.html`, and
only in the video's capture of that page (`capture.mjs` swaps the URLs). The
site itself still uses loremflickr; nothing here changes it.

Why not loremflickr: it serves Flickr Creative Commons images under mixed
licences, some non-commercial, and a capture of it is not guaranteed to be
repeatable. A paid ad needs both: commercial use, and the same frame every
render.
